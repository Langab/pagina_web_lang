The iPhone Health app stores everything it measures, since 2017 in my case. But it shows it to you by day, week or month. Never all nine years together. One Sunday in September curiosity won: what happens if I look at all of it at once, the way I'd look at a survey?

I requested the full export. A zip arrived with a 1.24 GB XML file. No spreadsheet opens that. With Claude Code as copilot, we turned it into a report within a few hours.

The full report also covers heart, sleep, blood oxygen and noise. Those charts stay between my watch and me. The GPS routes weren’t used either, because they draw where I live and how I move around.

Working with people’s data also means deciding what not to publish. That goes for a census and for my own phone.
